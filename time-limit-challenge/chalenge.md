Understand the problem and examples.

Minute 2–5:

Find the bottleneck.

Minute 10–12:
Derive the optimized approach.

Minute 12–15:
Code + test.

If you haven't solved it by minute 15, stop.

Your current situation sounds like 🟡 Yellow, and that's exactly what we want to turn into 🟢 Green.

One rule I REALLY want you to follow
When you solve a problem, don't immediately look at the optimal solution after getting a working solution.

Instead ask yourself these 4 questions:

What is my time complexity?

What part is causing the slowness?

Am I doing the same work repeatedly?

Can a Map, Set, pointer, sorting, or preprocessing eliminate that work?

That last step is where your interview improvement will come from.

Your target after 6 days
Don't aim for:

"I can solve every Medium in 15 minutes."

Aim for:

"Give me an unfamiliar Easy/Medium, and within 15 minutes I can explain my approach, identify its complexity, and know whether I need to optimize."

That's a much more realistic—and much more interview-useful—goal.
